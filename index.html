Source: Critical Mention exports for "Angie Nixon" and "Ashley Moody," October 5–26, 2026 (TV, radio, podcasts, online and print). Prepared by Technicolor Political.

Removed as ads: Moody campaign and Stronger, Safer Nation super PAC spots, Kalshi betting ads, and seven scripted Nixon profiles on Florida music stations that read as sponsored. Duplicate captures are also excluded.

October 6 caveat: Moody's positive share that day is lifted by one syndicated Clay Travis & Buck Sexton radio interview with Rick Scott (116 positive mentions, mostly outside Florida). Excluding it, her October 6 tone was 36% positive and 25% negative. On October 7, a syndicated Bill O'Reilly segment (116 airings, mostly out of state) drives Nixon's negative share; excluding it, she was 44% positive and 23% negative. Items about Richard Nixon were excluded.

How to read it: tone is Technicolor's read of how each item portrays that candidate, so one story can be positive for one and negative for the other. Radio counts each 60-second segment, as Critical Mention reports it. "Mentions both" means the same segment or article appears in both candidates' exports. The Mobile, AL DMA is counted as Florida (Pensacola). Online audience is outlet monthly visitors, not article readers.
